# QTON Regulatory Classification & Legal Compliance Memorandum

**Date:** September 10, 2026  
**Subject:** Regulatory Status & Compliance Classification of QTON Token  
**Jurisdiction:** Global Decentralized Standard (US Howey, EU MiCA, Singapore MAS Guidelines)  
**Standard:** TEP-74 Utility Jetton on TON Blockchain  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

QTON (Quantum TON) is architected as a **functional utility token and post-quantum cryptographic security layer** designed to facilitate decentralized computational coordination, automated cellular automaton entropy derivation, and launchpad governance on The Open Network (TON). 

Based on established regulatory frameworks, QTON qualifies as a **Utility Token** and does not constitute an investment contract or security.

---

## 2. Analysis Under the U.S. Howey Test

1. **Investment of Money:** 
   - QTON is distributed via community mining, testnet faucet allocation, and decentralized utility staking. There is no pooled initial coin offering (ICO) offering common enterprise rights.
2. **Common Enterprise:**
   - The token operates permissionlessly on TON. Smart contracts are governed by immutable 2-of-3 timelocked rules rather than centralized management discretion.
3. **Expectation of Profits Solely from the Efforts of Others:**
   - Token value derives from algorithmic utility: state attestation fees on the PQC Gateway, launchpad participation staking, and Conway automaton computational entropy fees. Users interact directly with smart contract execution.

## 3. EU Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) Classification

Under EU Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCA):
- QTON does NOT represent an Asset-Referenced Token (ART) or E-Money Token (EMT).
- QTON qualifies as a **Utility Token (Other Crypto-Asset)** intended to provide access to goods or services provided by the issuer or protocol network.

## 4. AML / CFT & Sanctions Compliance

- The protocol enforces non-custodial operations. All transfers adhere to TEP-74 transparent on-chain accounting.
- Web4 Terminal and Telegram Mini App endpoints prohibit interactions with sanctioned entities listed on OFAC, UN, and EU designated sanctions lists.
